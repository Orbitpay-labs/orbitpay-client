FAQs

Clear answers for Stellar checkout builders.

What does OrbitPay do?

OrbitPay helps Stellar and Soroban apps create simple checkout and C-address funding sessions. It shows users the amount, asset, destination, memo, and payment status in a cleaner flow.

Who is OrbitPay built for?

OrbitPay is built for Stellar app developers, wallet teams, merchants, and contributors who want cleaner checkout, USDC payment, and Soroban funding experiences.

Is OrbitPay replacing Stellar wallets?

No. OrbitPay works beside Stellar wallets. It packages payment instructions, memo details, C-address funding data, and status tracking into a checkout flow users can follow.

What can contributors build next?

Contributors can work on wallet adapters, Stellar payment watchers, webhook delivery, Soroban events, persistence, checkout polish, tests, and documentation for builders.
