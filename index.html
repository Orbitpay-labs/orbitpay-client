The friction

Standard payment flows fail on smart account onboarding.

OrbitPay sits between merchant apps, Stellar wallets, and Soroban smart accounts. It turns complex technical routing into a familiar, guided checkout experience.

🔴 High Friction

Direct Blockchain Transfer

  • Manual public key copy & paste
  • Easy to forget memo, risking lost funds
  • No real-time payment status tracking
  • Requires managing gas fees & trustlines
🟢 OrbitPay Path

Guided Onboarding Flow

  • 1-click copy details & deep-linking
  • Pre-filled memo verification built-in
  • Real-time UI & Webhook status updates
  • Gasless smart-account onboarding options
